"When did I last use this app?" "Which one is eating my storage?"
"Which apps can reach my microphone?"

AppShelf answers those three questions.

It is not a launcher and does not replace your home screen. It is a tool you open
a few times a year to take stock of what is installed.

■ What it does
・Lists, searches and sorts your installed apps
・Storage ranking with an app / data / cache breakdown
・Detects unused apps from last-used date and screen time
・Reverse lookup by sensitive permission (e.g. "8 apps can use the microphone")
・Tags, favourites and a hidden list for your own classification
・Uninstall selected apps one after another
・Long-term trend charts for size and screen time
・CSV / JSON export

■ Privacy
This app does not declare the internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET).
It is technically incapable of sending anything off your device.
No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no account required.

■ Permissions
・Usage access (optional)
  Used only to show last-used dates, screen time and app sizes.
  Without it you can still use the list, search, tags and permission views.
・Request delete packages
  Android always shows its own confirmation for each app.
  Silent or bulk uninstall is not possible on Android.

■ What it cannot do (Android restrictions)
・Revoke another app's permissions (it can only take you to that app's settings)
・Uninstall or disable system apps
・Uninstall apps without confirmation
・"Hidden" only affects lists inside this app, never your home screen

■ Technical notes
Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3 and Room.
App discovery uses the <queries> launcher-intent declaration rather than
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES. Source code is published under the Apache License 2.0.
